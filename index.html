😴 SleepScore — Sleep Quality Predictor

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A Ridge regression model trained on 600 synthetic sleep records predicts your sleep quality score (0–100) from sleep hours, screen time before bed, caffeine intake, exercise and stress level. 5-fold CV: R² = 0.846, MAE = 4.67 points vs a mean-guessing baseline of 12.06 points.

How it works: the inputs are standardised with the training means and standard deviations, multiplied by the model's learned coefficients, and clipped to 0–100. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try a sample: an average day — 7 hours sleep, 60 min screen, 150 mg caffeine, 30 min exercise, stress 5 → about 48.06. A great night — 8.5 hours sleep, 10 min screen, no caffeine, 60 min exercise, stress 2 → about 89.85. A rough night — 5 hours sleep, 150 min screen, 350 mg caffeine, no exercise, stress 9 → about 0.00.

This is a classroom demo on synthetic data — a fun guide to better sleep habits, not medical advice.
